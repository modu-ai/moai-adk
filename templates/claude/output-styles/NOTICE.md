# Adapted output styles

MoAI, MoAI-Easy and MoAI-Learn are modified, partial presentation adaptations of modu-ai/moai-adk, commit `4fa2aa9db2e0add0f1299e35407141b2b41c5ec0`, `.claude/output-styles/moai/`, licensed under Apache-2.0 (see LICENSE).

Changes: shortened presentation templates; execution and permission policies separated from presentation; coding/no-code mode distinction retained. These adaptations do not establish full behavioral equivalence. Source hashes and remaining differences are recorded in `docs/claude-style-crosswalk.md` at the repository root. Raw source remains local reference material and is not included in this new branch's selected product files.
