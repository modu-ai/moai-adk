+ ### Mode judgment — serial or parallelizable
+ Class names the stages a card needs; mode names whether it may run beside other cards. The two axes are independent. Decide the mode at issuance, from the card's expected file set — never from the queue's default. A card is `serial` only when it edits the same files or shared state as an in-flight card, or edits a slot-gated surface (factory/todo core, release pipeline, shared migrations); every other card is `parallelizable`. The reason recorded with the classification states the file set and the conflict check and, for a `serial` card, names the conflicting card (the conflict check names the shared state or the slot-gated surface it found). Supply the judgment with `--classification-file` (decider `human`); no card is issued on the fail-safe default. A card that reached the queue on the default is reissued with an explicit classification. For a Class C card, give `--spec` when the card is picked: it cannot be added after assign.

- - **Routing.** The leader routes a card WHOLE to a free lane — free means the lane's previous card reached `done` and its evidence was read. A lane busy on a card is not addressed; with every lane busy, the card waits in the queue rather than being dispatched. The address block carries `cmd`, which names the entry stage the class prescribes (`/moai plan` for C, `/moai run` for B, the direct close for A), and the lane proceeds through the remaining stages without further dispatches.

+ ### Delegated-dispatch promotion
+ Promotion remains an act of the operator. One further case is stated here, besides the `--auto` batch approval and a lane taking a lease itself: while the operator has delegated dispatch (operator away, or a standing delegation), the leader itself promotes queued cards to `picked` and assigns them, with no per-card operator pick. This section states every limit of that case, once.
+ - Where: only to a free live lane, as defined under Routing above.
+ - Which cards: only queued cards explicitly classified `parallelizable` by the mode judgment recorded at issuance (`factory-dispatch-cards.md` § Mode judgment).
+ - Order: queue priority and readiness first.
+ - Never promoted: a keep-set card, a held card, or a [보류-marked card (the keep-set is defined in `auto-semantics.md` §9.3).
+ - Serial cards: outside this case; a serial card follows the slot rules (`factory-dispatch-mechanics.md` § The leader's remaining role).
+ - Unchanged: lane self-dispatch through `moai factory next`.
+ Consequence, not a further limit: the assignment input of a free live lane is the picked cards plus, only while dispatch is delegated, the queued `parallelizable` cards.

- A lane renews its own card lease at the start of each watchdog pass with `moai factory renew <card>` (MCP tool `factory_renew`, the same implementation): the renewal extends the lease from the request time by `workflow.factory_lease.duration`, and a renewal of an expired lease is refused with no write.
+ While it executes the card, a lane renews its own card lease at the start of each watchdog pass with `moai factory renew <card>` (MCP tool `factory_renew`, the same implementation): the renewal extends the lease from the request time by `workflow.factory_lease.duration`, and a renewal of an expired lease is refused with no write.
+ ### Lease renewal — only the card being executed
+ Executing means the lane is carrying out a stage of the card — plan, run, sync, the card-review, or the landing sequence — and is not waiting on a slot, a trigger, or the operator.
+ A lane renews the lease of the card it is executing, and of no other card: only the card the lane is actually executing is renewed.
+ A lease on a merge-ready, held (operator-wait), or serial-waiting card may expire; expiry is harmless, because at the landing slot the leader reassigns the card or the lane re-leases it with `factory next --card`, the stage preserved.
+ The lane's standing recheck cron is kept while the lane executes the card; a lane waiting on a slot or a trigger arms and keeps no polling cron for that wait, and the leader sends a notice when a slot or a landing turn opens.
