## What the pages do not document

The web page's example `orca orchestration check --ack <deliveryId> --wait ...` is the older form; the installed CLI's own help says no `--ack` is needed. The pages and the local help are silent on: any mail state called stored, delivered or turn start, the field `delivered_at`, what a null of it means, and what happens to Runs, Tasks and mail after an Orca restart. A rule that needs any of these is written as measured on the local CLI and marked unverified, not as a documented fact.
