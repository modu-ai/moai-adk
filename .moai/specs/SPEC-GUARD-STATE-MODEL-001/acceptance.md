## §D.1 Criteria (Given-When-Then, two cells each)

### AC-GSM-001 — the manifest exists and its census is complete
**Given** the repository tree; **When** the manifest is read and its entry set compared against the workflow-file set on disk; **Then** an entry exists for every workflow file, with an empty set-difference in **both** directions.
- **RED-now:** measured on `52c3fe590` — `ls .moai/guards` returns `No such file or directory` (rc=1). Red because the artifact does not exist, not because a comparison failed. The disk side is measured: `ls -1 .github/workflows/*.yml .github/workflows/*.yaml | wc -l` → `18`.
- **Green path:** M1 populates the census. Passing output is an empty set-difference both ways.
- **Mutant:** a count-equality check is satisfied by 18 entries all naming the same file. The criterion is stated as a **set** comparison in both directions for that reason.

### AC-GSM-002 — every entry carries its five fields
**Given** the populated manifest; **When** each entry is checked for **kind**, locator, expected events, window, and measured quantity; **Then** BOTH hold — (a) no entry is missing any of the **five**, and (b) an entry deliberately constructed without a measured quantity, and separately one without a `kind`, are each **rejected with a named error** rather than defaulted.
- **RED-now:** no manifest and no reader exist on `52c3fe590` (AC-GSM-001's measurement). The rejection path cannot be exercised at all — there is nothing to reject with.
- **Green path:** M1. (a) passes on the census, (b) passes when the malformed entry produces the named error.
- **Mutant:** clause (a) alone is satisfied by a reader that silently defaults a missing measured quantity to `fired-at-all`. That mutant quietly converts a `skipped`-only subject into a green, which is the defect the vocabulary exists to catch.
- **Why the heading states five and is derived rather than transcribed:** the field count lives in REQ-GSM-002, and the heading is a restatement of it — so it is checked against its source (`grep -oE 'carry \*\*[a-z]+\*\* fields' spec.md`, and the enumeration in the `When` clause) rather than remembered. The v0.4.0 repair moved this criterion from four fields to five, corrected the body in three places, and left the heading reading **four** through v0.5.0, v0.6.0 and v0.7.0 — on the one criterion whose entire subject is a four-versus-five field count, where a reader skimming headings gets the number the repair removed.
- **Why `kind` is checked here and not only in the data-shape requirement:** row 1 of the state table decides on `kind`, so an entry missing it is **undecidable** — and an earlier draft listed four fields here while REQ-GSM-001 mandated `kind` elsewhere, so such an entry passed field-completeness and reached the classifier. A field the table decides on that the completeness check does not see is a gap between two requirements, not a wording preference.

### AC-GSM-003 — the measured-quantity vocabulary is closed and behaviourally distinct
**Given** the manifest reader and one recorded fixture holding a `skipped`, a `cancelled`, and a `success` run; **When** an entry declares each of the three values, and separately any fourth value; **Then** ALL THREE hold — (a) the three named values are accepted, (b) the fourth is rejected with a named error, and (c) **the three yield three different qualifying sets over that one fixture** — `fired-at-all` admits all three runs, `fired-with-effect` excludes the skipped and cancelled, `verdict-rendered` admits only the success.
- **RED-now:** no vocabulary and no reader exist (AC-GSM-001's measurement).
- **Green path:** M1 flips (a) and (b); M2 flips (c), which needs the classifier.
- **Mutant (a)/(b):** an accept-only criterion passes an all-accepting reader; a reject-only criterion passes a nothing-accepting one — the landed rule's own rule-pairing corollary.
- **Mutant (c):** (a) and (b) together are satisfied by a reader that accepts all three and treats them identically. That makes REQ-GSM-003's one-number-two-events prohibition unenforceable: an author could declare `verdict-rendered` and receive `fired-at-all` semantics, which is the conflation the requirement forbids.

### AC-GSM-007 — the state table is total: every row classifies
Stated as **three** independently-evaluable parts, because they are satisfiable at three different milestones and a compound `When` cannot be met at the earliest of them. Clause (d) joined at v0.7.0 and is the only one that reads what an entry *carries* rather than what the classifier *decides*.

**Clauses (a)(b) — requires the classifier. Given** a fixture supplying **one case per row** of the REQ-GSM-006 table (8 rows); **When** the evaluator classifies each case; **Then** BOTH hold — (a) every case receives **exactly one** classification, none unclassified and none receiving more than one, and (b) the classifier is table-driven, so each case's value traces to its row.

**Clause (c) — a schema read, requiring no evaluator. Given** the M1 schema and the table's `Flipped by` column; **When** the column is read against the schema; **Then** (c) **for every row** — not only rows that happen to declare a dependency — the M1 fields that row's decision depends on are present in the M1 schema.

**Clause (d) — the row-4 entry carries the expectation it missed. Given** the same per-row fixture and the manifest entry behind its **row-4** case; **When** the entry the evaluator emits for that case is read; **Then** (d) it carries **the declared expectation window** and **the declared measured quantity**, and each **equals the value on the manifest entry** — compared field-against-field, not checked for being non-empty.

- **RED-now (a)(b):** no classifier exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement). Red for absence — no case can be decided.
- **RED-now (c):** no manifest schema exists (AC-GSM-001's measurement), so no row's dependency can be present in it.
- **RED-now (d):** measured on `52c3fe590` — no evaluator emits an entry (AC-GSM-006's measurement), and the carried shape has nowhere to put one: `Entry` in `internal/guardliveness/contract.go` declares exactly three fields, `Subject`, `Classifications`, `Surface`. Red on **both** counts, and the second is the one that decides the milestone.
- **Green path:** **M1 flips (c)** — it is a schema read and needs no evaluator; **M2 flips (a)(b)**; **M3 flips (d)**. Passing output is 8 cases with 8 single classifications, every row's dependency present in the M1 schema, and the row-4 entry's carried window and measured quantity **equal to the manifest entry's**.
- **Why (d) is M3 and not M2:** the question *what does the classifier decide?* is M2's and (d) does not ask it — (d) asks *what does the emitted entry carry?*, and the carried shape is M3's deliverable (`plan.md` §F M3, `Emit guardliveness.Result in its carried shape`). Assigning (d) to M2 would repeat this criterion's own v0.4.0 defect from the other end: a clause scheduled at a milestone that cannot deliver it. This makes AC-GSM-007 the first criterion split across three milestones, which is the honest reading of a criterion whose three clauses ask three different questions.
- **Why the criterion is split:** an earlier draft carried one compound `Given/When` — "the evaluator classifies each case **and** the column is read against the schema" — while assigning clause (c) to M1, where no evaluator exists. The compound could not be satisfied at the milestone it was assigned to, and the green-path cell independently named M2 alone. **That is the predecessor's own failure shape appearing inside the criterion built to prevent it**: a clause scheduled where it cannot be evaluated. The split follows AC-GSM-003 and AC-GSM-005, which already carry per-clause milestones.
- **Why this criterion is the centre of the SPEC:** the predecessor's state model failed three audits by leaving a condition uncovered while every requirement read complete in isolation. A per-row fixture makes an uncovered condition a **failing case** rather than a sentence nobody wrote. This is the "state table, not prose" instruction made mechanical.
- **Mutant:** a classifier with a catch-all default satisfies "every case receives exactly one classification" while collapsing rows 1, 2 and 6 into one value. AC-GSM-008 is the paired direction that excludes it, and AC-GSM-009 and AC-GSM-010 pin the specific rows that have historically collapsed.
- **Delivery clause (c) is stated over ALL rows, not over rows that declare a dependency.** An earlier draft scoped it to "rows whose cell declares an M1 dependency (rows 1, 3, 4, 5)", which made the check **declaration-driven**: a row that under-declares its dependency is invisible to the very check the column exists to enable. Row 6 was exactly that case — the negative branch of row 5's conditional test, equally dependent on the same M1 field, declaring none. Scoping the clause over all rows removes the escape hatch; the row-6 cell is also now corrected.
- **Why clause (c) is a separate assertion from (a):** the predecessor's milestone map passed a **union count** — every criterion appeared in some flip list — while one criterion sat at a milestone that could not deliver it. A union count answers "is every criterion listed?" and is structurally blind to "can the listed milestone deliver it?". Clause (c) is the second question, and it is checked against the schema because that is the only place the answer lives.
- **Mutant (c):** clauses (a) and (b) together are satisfied by a table whose `Flipped by` column reads `M2` uniformly. That column would be true and useless — every classification is emitted by the classifier — and it would hide exactly the failure it exists to catch: a row whose M1 field was never shipped produces the **wrong value**, not a missing one. Row 5 is the sharp case: without its conditional field it silently becomes row 6, and a correctly-quiet release-only subject is reported as an anomaly on every sweep.
- **Mutant (d), and it is the reason clause (d) exists rather than the cell alone.** The mutant is a classifier that emits `STALE` for row 4 **and names nothing**. Walked across all 16 criteria on `52c3fe590`, it survives **every one of them**: AC-GSM-007(a) inspects *which* classification, not what the entry carries; AC-GSM-008 inspects *which values are reachable*; AC-GSM-013 is the only criterion that reads carried content and its subject is **result-level counts** (REQ-GSM-009), not per-entry data; the remaining thirteen read the manifest, the query path, the set comparison, the mutation surface, the designation, or the fold. Extending the row-4 cell without (d) would therefore have left the restored obligation **unfalsifiable** — present in the normative table, absent from verification, which is the exact shape the vacated clause had. With (d), the same mutant survives 15 criteria and is killed by (d) alone.
- **Mutant (d) — second, on (d)'s own wording.** A classifier carrying a constant string (`"expectation missed"`, or an empty-but-present field) satisfies any presence check. (d) is therefore stated as an **equality against the manifest entry's own two fields**, which is decidable by comparison and cannot be satisfied by a placeholder.
- **What (d) does not assert, recorded rather than quietly assumed:** it requires the *expectation*, not the *observation* — the entry need not carry how old the most recent qualifying run actually was. Naming the window without the age is half a comparison, and the residual is recorded in §D.7.

### AC-GSM-008 — every classification value is reachable
**Given** the same 8-row fixture; **When** the classifications are collected; **Then** **each of the seven values** — `OK`, `STALE`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNDECLARED`, `UNREADABLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, `ORPHANED` — is produced by at least one case.
- **RED-now:** no classifier exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement).
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output is all seven values observed across the 8 cases (rows 3 and 5 both yield `OK`).
- **Mutant this closes:** without it, **an evaluator that never emits `OK` passes every other criterion in this set** — AC-GSM-009 wants `UNRESOLVED`, AC-GSM-010 wants `UNKNOWN`, AC-GSM-011 wants `UNDECLARED`, and nothing requires a healthy subject to read clean. Such an evaluator marks every entry non-clean, so the consuming SPEC's advisory fires on every sweep with every entry in it — the always-red mechanism, arriving through the classifier.
- **Pairing:** AC-GSM-007 and AC-GSM-008 are the two directions of REQ-GSM-007's totality claim and are adopted as a pair. Either alone admits a mutant on the other direction.

### AC-GSM-009 — a failed query is `UNRESOLVED`, not silence (T2)
**Given** a manifest entry whose kind has a reader and whose query **returns an error** (simulated auth failure, then rate limit); **When** the evaluator classifies it; **Then** the classification is `UNRESOLVED`, it is **not** `UNKNOWN`, and it is counted in a per-value count of its own.
- **RED-now:** no classifier exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement). The condition itself is the audit's T2: under the predecessor's requirements this entry had **no admissible classification at all** — one requirement routed it into the no-reader value, another defined that value as no-reader-only, and the acceptance criteria assumed a branch the requirements forbade.
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output is `UNRESOLVED` for both error shapes.
- **Why the negative clause:** `UNKNOWN` means retention-bounded absence and its implied action is *look again with a longer window*, which is wrong advice for an auth failure. Two states with different implied actions under one label is the defect that produced this SPEC.
- **Note:** this is the single most likely degraded run in production, which is why it gets its own criterion rather than riding on AC-GSM-007's row coverage.

### AC-GSM-010 — excused absence is `OK`, aged or absent; unexcused absence is `UNKNOWN`
**Given** three entries — (i) and (ii) whose queries each return zero qualifying runs, one whose declared condition says firing is not currently expected and one whose condition does not account for the absence, and **(iii) an entry whose declared condition says firing is not currently expected and whose only qualifying run predates the expectation window**; **When** the evaluator classifies each; **Then** ALL THREE hold — (i) is `OK`, (ii) is `UNKNOWN`, and **(iii) is `OK` and is not `STALE`**, so the declared condition is consulted on the aged-run branch as well as on the zero-run branch and retention does not select the classification.
- **RED-now:** no classifier exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement).
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output is the two distinct classifications from the same observable input, **and `OK` for the third entry — not `STALE`**.
- **Why fixture (iii) exists, and it is a v0.8.0 finding from re-running the derivation rather than reading the table:** the entry axis consulted the declared condition only on the zero-qualifying-runs branch, so a declared-quiet entry whose last run is merely **aged** never reached the sentence that excuses it and classified `STALE`. That is the false-alarm direction on a healthy subject, on the two entries `AC-GSM-004` mandates — and **retention decided which label it got**, since the same real-world state reads `STALE` while the aged run is still visible and `OK` once retention consumes it. Fixture (iii) is the crossed case, and it was in no other criterion: **this criterion's own fixtures (i) and (ii) are zero-run entries by construction**, and `AC-GSM-007`'s per-row fixture supplies one case per row — so its row-4 case is a non-excused subject and its row-5 case was a zero-run one. Neither reached the cross.
- **Why both directions in one criterion:** the input is identical — zero runs — and only the declared expectation separates them. A criterion asserting either direction alone is satisfied by a classifier that ignores the declared condition entirely and always returns its one preferred value. This is also what keeps the consuming SPEC's advisory from firing every session on a healthy repository, so a collapse here is not local: it degrades the sibling SPEC's trigger into an always-red channel.

### AC-GSM-011 — an undeclared workflow file is reported
**Given** two fixtures exercising the set comparison in **both** directions — (i) a workflow file present on disk with no manifest entry, and (ii) a manifest entry whose named workflow file is absent from disk; **When** the evaluator runs on each; **Then** ALL hold — (i) classifies `UNDECLARED` and (ii) classifies **`ORPHANED`**, each appears in the output, each is counted, and **neither run reports an all-clear**.
- **RED-now:** no evaluator and no manifest exist (AC-GSM-006's measurement), so every one of the 18 files is undeclared and nothing reports any of them.
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output names the file and returns a non-all-clear result.
- **Mutant:** a criterion asserting only "appears in the output" is satisfied by an evaluator that lists it under a heading and still returns all-clear. The non-all-clear clause makes the classification consequential.
- **Why it is load-bearing:** this is the set comparison. An evaluator that reads outcomes only for entries the manifest already lists is the card's own defect at the evaluator's layer — correct about what it examined, silent about what it never examined.
- **Why fixture (ii) exists, and it is the totality hole this SPEC was built to make impossible:** an earlier draft covered the disk→entry direction only. The mirror — a declared entry whose subject was **deleted** — reached the classifier and was decided by run history, which outlives the deletion: it classified `STALE` (advice to investigate a subject that no longer exists) or, on a recent enough run, `OK` — **a false clean**. It did not present as an empty cell, which is why a totality claim over the table's own rows could not see it. Fixture (ii) is the runtime check that the second direction is actually implemented, not merely asserted at census time by AC-GSM-001.

### AC-GSM-012 — a degraded enumeration cannot report all-clear (T1)
**Given** two degraded enumerations over a fixture whose **every declared subject is present on disk** and whose run history would otherwise classify every entry `OK` — (i) one returning **zero files** (simulated wrong working directory, then a permissions failure), and (ii) one returning a **non-zero but partial** count (3 of 18, simulated wrong glob); **When** each run completes; **Then** ALL THREE hold — (a) **neither** run reports an all-clear, (b) the enumerated-files count is visible in each output, and (c) run (ii) is refused on the **enumeration-integrity check** — the corroborated declared-vs-enumerated comparison of REQ-GSM-010, not a zero test — **and no entry in either run classifies `ORPHANED`**, verified as a per-value count of zero rather than by inspecting the entries the fixture happens to name.
- **RED-now:** no evaluator and no enumeration exist (AC-GSM-006's measurement).
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output is a non-all-clear result with `enumerated: 0` visible.
- **Mutant this closes, and it is the card's own defect one layer in:** the set comparison has two inputs and the predecessor guarded only one. An evaluator whose enumeration silently returns empty classifies every manifest entry `OK`, reports `UNDECLARED: 0`, has a non-zero queried count so the zero-query guard does not bite, and **reports all-clear honestly about a set it never learned**. `UNDECLARED: 0` is indistinguishable between "census complete" and "enumeration found nothing" — clause (b) is what separates them.
- **Why AC-GSM-011 does not catch it:** that criterion's fixtures supply an undeclared file and an orphaned entry, so enumeration works there by construction.
- **Why clause (c) carries the `ORPHANED` half, and why the `Given` can stipulate `OK` again (v0.9.0):** at v0.8.0 this criterion asserted an outcome its own requirement could not produce and stipulated a fixture its own state table forbade. The entry axis led with an ungated disk-absence test, so on fixture (ii) the 15 entries the enumeration missed satisfied the leading split, were decided ahead of rows 1-6, and classified **`ORPHANED`** — making the `Given`'s *every entry otherwise `OK`* unsatisfiable — while REQ-GSM-010's tolerance term was *declared-minus-enumerated beyond what `UNDECLARED` and `ORPHANED` account for*, which those same 15 findings exactly consumed: 18 − 3 = 15, `UNDECLARED` 0, `ORPHANED` 15, **15 is not more than 15 and the guard did not fire**. The integrity gate (REQ-GSM-006, REQ-GSM-010) closes both halves at once: the corroborating point test finds all 15 subjects present, so integrity fails, row 8 is suppressed for every entry, the 15 route onward and reach row 3 `OK` as the `Given` stipulates, and the refusal is carried by the integrity check rather than by a term the degradation computed. Clause (c) asserts **both** — the refusal and the absence of `ORPHANED` — because either alone is satisfied by a repair that fixes one and relocates the other.
- **Why fixture (ii) exists — a zero-check is the card's own defect at a lower dose:** an enumeration returning 3 of 18 files is non-zero, so a zero-guard passes it unremarked. It yields no `UNDECLARED` finding for the 15 files it never saw and reports all-clear about a set that had silently become the wrong set. The guard therefore has to test that the **right** thing happened, not merely that *something* did — the same proxy failure as the parent card's `queried < declared` trigger. It is nearly free, because REQ-GSM-009 already carries both counts.

### AC-GSM-013 — every carried count has a consumer
**Given** the evaluator's result; **When** it is emitted; **Then** BOTH hold — (a) it carries the measurement timestamp, the enumerated-files count, the declared count, the queried count, and a per-value count for each of the seven classifications, and (b) **each carried count is consumed by a named consumer that reads it** — a decision it changes, or a guard condition it feeds — with no count that is rendered and read by nothing.
- **RED-now:** no evaluator exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement).
- **Green path:** M3. Passing output is the full count set with each count traced to a consumer.
- **Why clause (b):** the parent card's audit found no inert requirement but **two inert fields** — counts required, rendered, and consumed by nothing. Field-level inertness inside a live requirement is the residue this clause prevents, and the enumerated-files count is a count with a consumer by construction (REQ-GSM-010's guard reads it, and now compares it against the declared count).
- **The disjunct that had to go, because it made the clause vacuous:** an earlier draft accepted a count as consumed if it was "named in a decision, a guard condition, **or the published result's own contract**". Every carried count is by definition part of the published result, so the third disjunct is satisfied by any emitted field — and it contradicted REQ-GSM-009, which says a count *rendered* and consumed by nothing is exactly the inertness to avoid. Mutant it admitted: emit all seven per-value counts plus the timestamp, declare each "consumed by the published result's own contract", pass the criterion with every count inert. The clause now requires a consumer that **reads** the count.

### AC-GSM-014 — the evaluator mutates nothing
**Given** the evaluator and a fixture whose manifest contains at least one `STALE` and one `UNDECLARED` entry — the classifications most likely to tempt an action; **When** one full evaluation runs; **Then** BOTH hold — (a) **zero** mutating forge calls (no issue creation, no comment, no dispatch, no re-run), counted the same way AC-GSM-006 counts queries, and (b) the working tree is byte-identical before and after, verified by a **content-bearing comparison** — a path+hash manifest over the tree excluding `.git`, taken immediately before and after the run and compared, or the equivalent `find . -newer <stamp> -type f -not -path './.git/*'` sweep anchored on a stamp created immediately before it, asserting an empty result **over the fixture tree the evaluator is run against, not over the development checkout** — with `git status --porcelain --ignored` retained as the **tracked-path half** only.
- **RED-now:** no evaluator exists (AC-GSM-006's measurement) — there is no call path to count.
- **Green path:** M3. Passing output is a zero mutating-call count and an unchanged tree.
- **Mutant:** clause (a) alone is satisfied by an evaluator writing its cache into the working tree — no forge mutation, but a git write that becomes drift for the next reader. Clause (b) alone is satisfied by an evaluator that opens an issue and touches no file.
- **Why the instrument is content-bearing, and this is the second time this criterion's own mutant escaped it.** The mutant is stated: an evaluator persisting its cache to a gitignored path inside the tree — `.moai/state/…` is the natural choice, and `plan.md` §D contemplates exactly this location — writes no tracked file, passes a porcelain check, and violates REQ-GSM-011's "shall not write to the repository working tree". At v0.4.0 the instrument moved from `git status --porcelain` to `--ignored`, which closed the *narrow* case: a cache at a newly-ignored path does produce a fresh `!!` line. **Measured on `52c3fe590`, in this tree, this run, it does not close the case the mutant actually takes.** `git status --porcelain --ignored` collapses an ignored *directory* to a single entry — `!! .moai/state/context-usage/` and `!! .moai/logs/` are two such **directory** entries here, and the figure is deliberately left underived because it is volatile: the listing is `git status --porcelain --ignored | grep '^!!'`, its directory subset `… | grep '/$'`, and both move whenever an ignored file is written, which is what this note is about — so a write **inside** a collapsed directory produces no delta at all — probed: baseline captured, a file created under an already-collapsed ignored directory, `diff` of the two porcelain listings → rc=0, no difference; the file then grown from 6 bytes to 41 → `diff` again rc=0. **The general form is what the fix addresses, and it is stronger than the measured instance:** `git status` reports path *status*, never file *content*, so it cannot decide "byte-identical" for any ignored path under any flag combination — the modify probe demonstrates that directly. The replacement was probed against the same escape on the same tree: `find . -newer <stamp> -type f -not -path './.git/*'` returned exactly the file the porcelain listing had missed. `--ignored` is kept for the tracked-path half, where it is sound; it is no longer asked to decide the clause. **The empty-result assertion is stated over the fixture tree deliberately (v0.9.0):** a `-newer` sweep reports every writer, not only the one under test, and a probe of it in this development checkout returned the planted file **and** `./.moai/logs/trace-<session>.jsonl`, written by the harness. Under a live checkout the assertion is unachievable and the implementer meets a false red; over a fixture tree with no concurrent writer it is exactly right. The reading was always intended and was never stated, which is the whole of the correction.

### AC-GSM-015 — the published contract holds
**Given** any emitted result, **and the same result after a save-then-load cycle through the store** (`internal/guardliveness/store.go`), so both the in-memory value and its round-tripped twin are inspected; **When** their classifications and their designation are inspected; **Then** ALL THREE hold — (a) every entry carries exactly one classification, (b) **exactly one value in the vocabulary is designated clean**, verified by asserting the designation is single-valued rather than by counting how many entries happen to be `OK`, and (c) **the result carries that designation in machine-readable form**, such that a consumer given only the result — and no knowledge of any value's name — can partition entries into clean and non-clean. Clause (c) is mechanized the way its mirror is: the partitioning test harness contains **no classification value token**, verified by a value-token grep over that harness returning rc=1, so the harness's blindness is measured rather than asserted.
- **RED-now:** no evaluator and no published designation exist (AC-GSM-006's measurement).
- **Green path:** M3. Passing output is a single designated clean value.
- **Why this criterion exists here and not in the consuming SPEC:** `SPEC-GUARD-LIVENESS-001` consumes the contract and can verify that its own trigger uses the clean/non-clean partition — but it **cannot** verify that exactly one clean value exists, because it never names the vocabulary. If this SPEC ever designated two clean values, every criterion in the consuming SPEC would still pass while its advisory under-fired. This criterion is the other half of that seam.
- **Mutant (b):** clause (b) stated as "at least one entry classifies `OK`" is satisfied while a second value is also treated as clean. Asserting the **designation** rather than the observed entries is what closes it.
- **Mutant (c), and it is the seam's own failure mode:** clauses (a) and (b) together are satisfied by a result that designates the clean value **in prose in this SPEC** and emits nothing. The consumer is then forced to hardcode the literal — which its own criteria forbid — or to read the surface fold, which under-reports because `UNREADABLE` also folds to `ok` while only `OK` is clean. Clause (c) is what makes the designation a *carried field* rather than a documented fact, and the test is stated as *a consumer given only the result and no value names can partition it*, so it is decidable without reference to either SPEC's prose.
- **Why the round trip is in the `Given` (v0.9.0):** `plan.md` M3 names the store round trip load-bearing twice and says to *"verify by save-then-load, not by inspecting the in-memory struct"*, and no criterion bound it — the obligation lived only in the plan, where nothing fails if it is skipped. That is F2's shape one layer down, and it lands hardest on clause (d)'s **additive expectation field**, the newest member of the carried shape and the one whose JSON tag `plan.md` itself flags as the hazard: a field with a wrong or missing tag arrives at the consumer as a contract violation that never happened. Widening the `Given` binds every clause of this criterion — and, through the shared result, clause (d)'s carried fields — to the serialized form rather than the in-memory one. One clause, no new criterion.
- **Symmetry note:** this clause is the producing half of a two-sided obligation. Its consuming half is `SPEC-GUARD-LIVENESS-001` REQ-GDL-001 (iii)/(iv) and AC-GDL-001(c). Repairing one side alone re-opens the gap in the other direction, which is why both landed in the same commit.

### AC-GSM-016 — every classification folds, and none folds to `fail`
**Given** the 8-row fixture of AC-GSM-007 plus the table's `Surface fold` column; **When** each case is classified and folded; **Then** ALL THREE hold — (a) every one of the seven classifications' fold **equals the value in the `Surface fold` column of the REQ-GSM-006 table** — an equality over all seven, compared value-against-column, not a check that some fold exists — (b) **no classification folds to `fail`**, and (c) the meaningless/incomplete boundary holds — `UNREADABLE` folds to `ok` while `UNKNOWN`, `UNRESOLVED` and `ORPHANED` fold to `warn`.
- **RED-now:** no classifier and no fold exist on `52c3fe590` (AC-GSM-006's measurement). Red for absence. The surface vocabulary the fold targets is measured on the **same tree** — `grep -nE '^\s+Check(OK|Warn|Fail) CheckStatus' internal/cli/uikit/types.go` returns `CheckOK`/`CheckWarn`/`CheckFail`, three values with no skipped state. *(At v0.5.0 this cell pinned `origin/develop` at `ec15ec2cd` as a diverged tree; that tree is an ancestor of `52c3fe590` and the surface is readable here, so the second pin is retired — `spec.md` §C.2.1.)*
- **Green path:** M2. Passing output is a fold for **each of the seven classification values**, each equal to its row's `Surface fold` cell, zero `fail`, and the boundary as stated. **The figure is a count of values, not of rows**, and the two differ — 7 against 8, because rows 3 and 5 both classify `OK` — so the cell names which it means and derives it: values, `awk -F'|' '/^  \| [0-9] \|/ {gsub(/[ `]/,"",$4); print $4}' spec.md | sort -u | wc -l` → **7**; rows, for the separate row count, `grep -c '^  | [0-9] |' spec.md` → **8**. The never-`fail` clause is derivable from the same table: `awk -F'|' '/^  \| [0-9] \|/ {gsub(/[ `]/,"",$5); print $5}' spec.md | sort -u` → `ok`, `warn`. *(Corrected at v0.6.0: this cell read "six declared folds", the pre-`ORPHANED` figure, while clause (a) already said seven. Corrected again at v0.8.0: the derivation the v0.6.0 fix cited to prevent recurrence, `grep -c '^| [0-9]' <table>`, matches the **HISTORY rows** — the state-table rows are indented two spaces, so `^|` never reaches them. It returned 7 and agreed with the asserted figure by coincidence, and would have read 8 the moment this very version's HISTORY row landed, at which point it would have "confirmed" the wrong artifact. A derivation that measures the wrong thing, cited in a cell whose subject is that transcribed numbers drift, is worse than the number it replaced.)*
- **Mutant (b):** without the never-`fail` clause, an implementation folding `UNDECLARED` or `STALE` to `fail` satisfies every other criterion while promoting a routine sweep to a failing exit status. Card t326 reached the same conclusion for the same reason and recorded it in source: *"never Fail — a Fail here would promote every downstream `moai doctor` run to exit 1"*.
- **Mutant (c), and it is this SPEC's own subject:** an implementation that adopts t326's leniency wholesale folds **all** uncertainty to `ok` — `UNREADABLE`, `UNKNOWN` and `UNRESOLVED` alike. It passes (a) and (b), reads as principled reuse, and reproduces the defect this card exists to catch: a mechanism reporting green about a set it never learned. Clause (c) is where the reuse stops, and it stops at the line between *meaningless* (no reader — the comparison never applied) and *incomplete* (the comparison applied and could not finish).

## §D.7 Residual risk (recorded, not claimed as closed)

- **The state table is total over the two inputs the derivation names, which is not the same as total over reality.** AC-GSM-007 proves every row classifies and AC-GSM-008 proves every value is reachable; neither proves the eight rows exhaust what the mechanism can encounter. **This bound is not theoretical — it has already been hit once.** The v0.2.0 table asserted totality over seven rows and a by-construction re-derivation broke it immediately at the deleted-subject condition, which produced a wrong value rather than an empty cell. The improvement is that the derivation method is now stated beside the table (REQ-GSM-006), so a reader can re-run it instead of trusting the result; the residual is that a *third input* would open conditions this derivation cannot reach, and nothing here would surface that.

  **The third-input question was asked at v0.6.0 and answered, so it is now a measured bound rather than an open worry.** The landed consumer persists each result and renders it at a later activation, which makes the persisted prior result — and the *"the advisory may be one activation stale"* property that follows from it — the obvious third-input candidate. **Measured on `52c3fe590`: it is not one.** `guardliveness.Activation` carries exactly one field (`Root`), and `Producer.Produce(ctx, act)` receives no prior result, so nothing persisted can reach a classification decision. The prior result is read by the **render** — to lead with what changed and to state the measurement's age — which is surfacing, and out of scope (`spec.md` §D). The store round trip is therefore a third input to the *surfacing* layer, and the seam is what keeps it out of this one. **The table was not extended**, and the bound is now specific: a third classification input would have to arrive as a new field on `Activation` or a new source the producer reads directly, which is a visible signature change rather than a silent drift.
- **The v0.6.0 re-derivation found a defect the table's own totality proof could not see, and the same blind spot remains.** AC-GSM-007 proves every row classifies and AC-GSM-008 proves every value is reachable — neither says anything about **which** row an entry reaches when more than one row's condition holds. That is decided by the split order, which is prose in REQ-GSM-006 rather than a column of the table, and the v0.6.0 finding (row 8 versus row 2 on an errored query for a deleted subject) lived exactly there: the table was complete and the reading order was wrong. Row 8's `Entry condition` cell now carries its precedence, but **no criterion asserts the ordering**, because doing so would need a fixture entry satisfying two rows at once and the AC budget is at its ceiling. AC-GSM-011 fixture (ii) is the nearest guard and does not cover the double-condition case.
- **A third classification input would arrive as a signature change, not silently — but nothing checks for it.** The bound above is measurable today; nothing re-measures it. A later change adding a field to `Activation`, or a producer reading the store directly, would open conditions the eight rows cannot reach, and no criterion here would fail.
- **`UNREADABLE`'s distinctness rests on one axis only.** Its implied action ("None until a reader exists") is operationally identical to `OK`'s ("None"), and both fold to `ok`. What separates them is **cleanliness**: `OK` is the clean value and `UNREADABLE` is not, carried to consumers by the designator (REQ-GSM-012). REQ-GSM-007's distinctness clause is worded over *observable axes* for this reason rather than over implied actions, which the pair would have satisfied only in wording. The value is not inert — it is the sole classification that is non-clean while folding to the clean surface value, which is exactly the gap entry the sibling's own seam criterion requires as a fixture — but its work is carried by one axis, and if the designator were ever dropped the pair would become genuinely indistinguishable.
- **A second-kind entry would be permanently `UNREADABLE`.** No second-kind reader ships (§D Out of Scope), so such an entry is a standing non-clean entry whose implied action is "None" — the permanently-non-clean neighbour the sibling SPEC warns trains a reader's filter. Nothing here prevents one being declared. **And v0.6.0 added a second hazard on the same axis:** the entry axis now leads with a disk-absence test, which is guarded on the kind being disk-enumerable precisely so a non-workflow entry is not classified `ORPHANED` for failing to appear in a workflow-file enumeration. The guard is vacuous while one kind ships, and it is stated rather than measured — no criterion exercises it, because exercising it needs a second kind that does not exist. **And the guard's two properties are independent, which leaves one row pair whose precedence the table does not state.** "Disk-enumerable" and "has a reader" are separate: a future kind that is disk-enumerable but readerless satisfies row 8's leading split *and* row 1's condition for a deleted subject. The split order decides it row 8, and that is the **right** answer — `ORPHANED` / *reconcile the manifest* beats `UNREADABLE` / *do nothing* for a subject that is gone — so this is a recorded bound rather than a defect. It is noted beside the vacuity because it is the one remaining place on the entry axis where two rows can both apply and the precedence is carried only by the leading split's position, not by either cell: row 8's cell states precedence over rows 1-6 collectively, and row 2's states its own, while row 1's says nothing.
- **The enumeration-integrity check is independent of the enumeration's *pattern*, not of its *root*.** The corroborating point test reads the entry's own locator and does not re-run the glob, which is what makes it independent of the v0.9.0 defect's cause (a wrong pattern returning 3 of 18) and of any traversal or filter error in the same class. It resolves that locator against **the same root** the enumeration used, so a root-level error is not caught by corroboration: the point test would agree the subject is absent, and the entries would classify `ORPHANED` on evidence that is wrong twice. REQ-GSM-010's zero-file clause closes the realistic form of this — a wrong root enumerates nothing — and it is stated as an *integrity failure* rather than only as an all-clear refusal precisely so row 8 is suppressed there too. **What remains uncovered is a root that is wrong and non-empty** — a sibling checkout, a stale worktree — where every declared subject genuinely is absent from the tree being read. Nothing here distinguishes that from a real mass deletion, and no criterion exercises it; the honest bound is that the integrity check verifies the enumeration was *complete for the tree it read*, not that it read the *right* tree.
- **The restored row-4 obligation stops at *carried*, and nothing yet shows it to an operator.** Clause (d) asserts the emitted entry carries the missed window and measured quantity. It does not assert they are displayed, and on `52c3fe590` they would not be: both of the consumer's render sites print the subject alone (`internal/guardliveness/advisory.go:94` and `Render` in `contract.go:160`), so the carried fields arrive and are dropped at the render. The display half belongs to the sibling's surfacing requirements and is out of scope here (`spec.md` §D) — which means the pre-split clause's *operator-facing* intent is **half-discharged by this SPEC and half unowned by either**. This is a narrower gap than the one F2 found, and it is a gap: it should be raised against the sibling rather than closed here.
- **Clause (d) obliges an additive field on a type this SPEC does not own.** `guardliveness.Entry` has three fields and no place for an expectation, so M3 must add one to a struct in the sibling's package, with its JSON tag load-bearing for the store round trip. The addition names no classification value, so §B's stated invariant (*a change to the vocabulary's size or to any value's name requires no change to this package*) holds by its letter — but it is a cross-SPEC write, the second M3 now carries alongside replacing the stub line. **Both writes are now named in the SPEC's `module:` frontmatter, which at v0.7.0 named neither.** It read `.moai/guards, internal/cli, internal/guard`: `internal/guard` **does not exist** (`ls -d internal/guard` → rc=1, measured on `52c3fe590`), `internal/guardliveness` was conceded here and nowhere else, and **`internal/hook` was recorded nowhere at all** — M3 replaces exactly one line there, `internal/hook/session_start_guard_liveness.go:38`, the single production `guardliveness.Unwired()` site (`grep -rn "guardliveness.Unwired()" internal/ pkg/ cmd/` → one match). `internal/cli` was dropped on evidence rather than kept by inheritance: no milestone in `plan.md` §F writes it, and the only `internal/cli` mentions there are a t326 citation (B-7) and the pre-flight row measuring the **absence** of an evaluator verb, which is a RED-now measurement and not a deliverable commitment. The field now names the measured write set.
- **Clause (d) requires the expectation and not the observation.** The row-4 entry must carry the window it fell outside; it need not carry how far outside. An operator given the window without the age has the expectation and not the measurement — half the comparison — and nothing here requires the other half. Adding it would need either a third carried field or a widened clause, and the AC budget cannot absorb a criterion for it.
- **The carried expectation is a field whose consumer is not code.** `Entry.Surface` already sits in this position and §D.7 records it; clause (d) adds a second. AC-GSM-013's every-count-has-a-consumer clause binds **counts**, not per-entry fields, so neither is caught by it. Two fields emitted for a reader who cannot currently see them is the nearest thing in this SPEC to the field-level inertness B-4 exists to prevent, and it is stated rather than defended.
- **`gh run list` retention bounds the design from above.** REQ-GSM-006 row 6 converts retention loss into an explicit `UNKNOWN`, preventing a false alarm. It does not recover the lost history: a subject that stopped firing longer ago than the retained window reads `UNKNOWN` indefinitely. An expectation window exceeding retention is therefore not answerable by this design, and the manifest does not currently reject one.
- **Row 5 depends on the declared condition being written correctly.** A release-only entry whose condition is mis-authored classifies `OK` while genuinely stale — a false clean, which is the worst direction. AC-GSM-004 checks the condition is *present*, not that it is *right*, and nothing here can check the latter.
- **The manifest is hand-maintained.** AC-GSM-011 catches a workflow file added without an entry only when the evaluator next runs, and reports it as `UNDECLARED` rather than blocking. A new guard therefore sits undeclared until someone reads the result. Deliberate — a blocking completeness gate would be a new always-green risk — but it is a gap.
- **`UNREADABLE` is asserted by one criterion and exercised by no shipping code path.** No second-kind reader ships (§D Out of Scope), so the value's only exercise is AC-GSM-005's shape fixture. It is reachable per AC-GSM-008, but its implied action has never been acted on.
- **The evaluator's own liveness is out of this SPEC's hands.** Whether it runs at all is `SPEC-GUARD-LIVENESS-001`'s REQ-GDL-003. A correct state model invoked by nothing produces no classifications, and nothing here would detect that — the split places this SPEC's own liveness in its sibling, which is the honest consequence of the seam.
