- Spawn multiple subagents in one turn when fanning out across independent items or files; never spawn one for work completable in a single response
- Three primitives — **sub-agents**, **Agent Teams**, **dynamic workflows** — chosen by who holds the plan; coding-heavy work prefers sequential sub-agents, workflow-scale fan-out is for genuinely parallel high-volume tasks.
